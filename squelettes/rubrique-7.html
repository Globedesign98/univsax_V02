Registrations are accepted until 15 June 2026, subject to availability.

The 37th edition of the European University of Saxophone will take place from Monday 13 July to Thursday 23 July 2026.

FEES: €1028

The fee includes registration for the pedagogical courses (€500) as well as (optional) accommodation with full board at the Foyer des Jeunes Travailleurs in Gap for the duration of the course (€528). The cost of full‑board accommodation (breakfast, lunch and dinner) is €49.30 per day. (for indication only; no possibility of splitting).

FEES: €500 (without accommodation)

FEES: €250 (Auditors)

In case of cancellation, a refund may be issued (a €130 administration fee will be retained). No refunds after 10 June 2026.
